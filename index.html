FAQs

Questions Before You Start

Straight answers on cost, timelines and what is actually covered.

What does an interior project cost?
Cost depends on carpet area, the scope you choose and the materials you select — a modular kitchen alone sits in a very different range to a full turnkey home. Share your layout and we will give you an itemised, no-obligation estimate.
Is the design consultation free?
Yes. Talking to our interior designer and getting an initial estimate costs you nothing and carries no obligation.
Do I get to see the design before work starts?
Yes. We produce detailed drawings and 3D visualization for your approval before anything goes into production — we have delivered 300+ 3D renders to date.
Which areas do you serve?
Our studio is in Raj Nagar Extension, Ghaziabad, and we take on residential and commercial projects across Ghaziabad and Delhi NCR.
Do you handle renovation and civil work?
Yes. Alongside interiors we take on renovation and construction, bathroom and kitchen renovation, false ceiling, wallpaper and wall decor, and complete building design.
Do you do commercial and office interiors?
Yes. Office interiors and commercial space interiors are a core part of what we do, in addition to full home interiors.
What should I share to get an accurate estimate?
Your floor plan or room measurements, which rooms you want designed, the style you like and a rough budget range. If you do not have a plan yet, tell us your property type and locality and we will guide you from there.
How long will my project take?
Timelines depend on scope and site conditions. Your project manager will commit to a milestone-tracked schedule at the time of approval.
